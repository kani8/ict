## Studies and predeclared designs

### M1 — Liquidity magnetism (tasked first)

**Claim under test**: "price seeks liquidity" — the presence of a resting
pool makes a move toward it more likely than an equal-sized move made
unconditionally.

- **Events**: every confirmed liquidity pool; at its confirm bar record
  side, distance to level in ATR units, and whether the level is reached
  within horizon N (its `taken_index`).
- **Baseline**: for each event, K = 20 random bars matched on ATR
  (±25%), measuring the probability of an equal-sized move in the same
  direction within the same horizon.
- **Statistic**: touch-rate / baseline-rate ratio per predeclared
  distance bucket {0.5–1, 1–2, 2–4 ATR} × horizon {96, 384 bars}, with
  block-bootstrap CIs over time-ordered events.
- **Reading rule (declared now)**: the claim is supported in a cell only
  if the ratio CI excludes 1.0 upward; "magnetism is real" requires
  supported cells in ≥ 2 of 3 datasets for the same bucket/horizon.
  Implementation: `analytics/magnetism.py` (tested library code; the
  executor runs it, never re-implements it).

### M2 — Post-sweep conditional behavior (not yet tasked)

Forward return AND realized-volatility distributions after sweep vs run
vs matched no-event bars (vol- and killzone-matched). Tests whether
sweeps carry direction, volatility expansion, or nothing.

### M3 — High-volume revisit (not yet tasked)

After a volume-spike bar (volume > trailing-30d q99) that price leaves
by ≥ 1 ATR, does price revisit the spike bar's midpoint within N bars
more often than matched non-spike bars? Directly tests the user's
"markets revisit high-volume events" intuition.
