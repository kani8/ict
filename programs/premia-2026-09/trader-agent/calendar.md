## D. Monthly options expiration and quarterly futures roll/expiry (Sep-Dec 2026)

Computed as the 3rd Friday of each month (standard US equity/index options
and CME equity-index futures expiration convention). Quarterly futures
(ES=F, NQ=F contract roll) expire on the 3rd Friday of Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec.

| Month | 3rd Friday (monthly opex) | Quarterly futures expiry/roll |
|---|---|---|
| Sep 2026 | Sep 18, 2026 | Sep 18, 2026 (quarterly) |
| Oct 2026 | Oct 16, 2026 | - |
| Nov 2026 | Nov 20, 2026 | - |
| Dec 2026 | Dec 18, 2026 | Dec 18, 2026 (quarterly) |

Note: actual CME futures last-trade dates can be a session or two before
expiry (e.g. ES/NQ last trade is the trading day before the 3rd Friday);
treat the 3rd Friday as the roll/rebalance reference point and re-check
cmegroup.com contract specs before rolling a live position near those
dates.
